Custom draft marking

For an unusual vessel, define the visible labels line by line. Each row is value,label. Value is the draught in metres represented by the bottom edge of that character/mark. Left, Right and Alternate alignment are now honored exactly.

Purpose

This lets you recreate a non-standard or unfamiliar scale without changing the simulation engine. Enter the actual numerical reference of each visible mark, then practice against it.

The trainer intentionally treats this as visual practice, not as an official vessel measurement or survey instrument.

How to use the trainer

Watch the water intersection against the draught marks for several wave cycles. Do not chase a single crest or trough. Estimate the underlying mean level, enter your reading, and commit it.

Difficulty and peeled paint: Easy keeps the draft marks clean. Normal introduces light wear, Hard removes more paint, and Expert can heavily obscure parts of the scale. You can override this behavior in Settings.

Metric preset: each numeral is calibrated to 10 cm physical height and displayed mark bottoms are 20 cm apart. The lower edge/reference line is the stated even-decimetre draught; the top edge is +10 cm. Example: bottom of the 8 mark = x.80 m, top = x.90 m, and the bottom of the next whole-metre mark = (x+1).00 m.

Imperial presets: use six-inch character increments/gaps for visual practice. The Roman option changes the presentation style. Custom mode can reproduce an unfamiliar scale you encounter.

Round timing: A new scenario begins with a configurable observation countdown (10 s default). The answer field stays locked until GO. Decision time starts at GO. The default late-score threshold is 30 s. After committing, the result remains visible for the configured auto-next delay (10 s default), then a new scenario begins. On a keyboard, Enter commits and Space starts a new scenario.

Viewing distance: Near, Normal, Far and Very Far reduce or enlarge the apparent ship/mark scale while keeping the simulated draught geometry unchanged. This is a visual-acuity training aid, not a calibrated real-world distance measurement.

Sea model: the display combines long swell, multiple short waves, seeded pseudo-random noise, grouped waves, transient high crests and deep troughs, and optional vessel heave/roll. It is intentionally an educational visual model rather than a hydrodynamic prediction.

Important: use actual approved vessel markings, survey procedures and professional judgment for real draft surveys. This game is for eye-training only.
